How FAIR Works

Risk Decomposition

FAIR breaks risk into Loss Event Frequency (how often) and Probable Loss Magnitude (how much), giving precise, actionable numbers.

Monte Carlo Simulation

Instead of a single point estimate, we run 10,000 simulations using PERT distributions to capture full uncertainty ranges.

Dollar-Quantified Output

Risk is expressed in Annual Loss Exposure (ALE) — a dollar range that maps directly to business decisions and ROI calculations for controls.

Threat Event Frequency

TEF represents how many times per year a threat agent acts against your asset, regardless of whether they succeed.

Consider:

  • How often does the threat community target organisations like yours?
  • Does your asset's visibility (internet-exposed, public profile) increase contact?
  • Industry threat intelligence, breach reports, and red team data can calibrate this.

Use 0.1 for once every 10 years. Use 365 for daily attacks.

Vulnerability

Vulnerability is the probability that a threat event results in a loss — i.e., that the attacker succeeds.

Loss Magnitude

Estimate the dollar impact per individual loss event. Ask yourself:

  • Productivity: If systems are down for X hours, what's the revenue/cost impact?
  • Response: Forensics firm ($150–250/hr), internal IR team overtime, external comms?
  • Replacement: Rebuild servers, restore data, re-license software?
  • Fines: GDPR max 4% global revenue; HIPAA up to $1.9M per violation category.
  • Competitive: If IP is stolen, what's the 3-year revenue impact?
  • Reputation: Customer churn × LTV, PR spend, stock impact.
